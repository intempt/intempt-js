Commerce events

Three methods, three different argument shapes. That is history rather than design, so check the table before writing the call.

CallArgumentEvent title sent
productView(id)a plain stringProduct viewed
productAdd(product)one object { productId, quantity? }Added to cart
productOrdered(products)an array of those objectsProduct ordered

None of the three validate their arguments. A missing or misspelled productId is accepted and sent, so whatever you pass is what your reports will have to live with. Validate before you call.

Where identity fits

Commerce events attach to the current profile. To tie an order to a known customer, call identify({ userId }) before the order event — at sign-in is the natural place, not at checkout. If you only learn the email at checkout, identify with your own stable customer ID and pass the email as a userAttributes field alongside an eventTitle.
