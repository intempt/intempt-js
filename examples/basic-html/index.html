Automatic tracking

You did not wire any of this up. Click the button, type in the field, or submit the form and watch the log: the SDK captures clicks, field changes and submits on its own, plus a page view when this page loaded.

The second field carries the doNotCapture attribute, so its displayed text is masked. It does not strip the value submitted through the form — do not rely on it for whole-form secrecy.
